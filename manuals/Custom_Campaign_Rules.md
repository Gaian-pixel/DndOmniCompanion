## Potions as a Bonus Action
Drinking a healing potion yourself requires only a **Bonus Action** instead of a Standard Action.
Administering a potion to an unconscious or downed ally still requires a **Standard Action**.

## Brutal Critical Hits
When landing a Critical Hit (Natural 20), rather than rolling all damage dice twice, you maximize the first set of weapon damage dice and roll the second set normally, adding standard modifiers.
*Example*: Greatsword (2d6) with +3 STR -> 12 maximized + 2d6 rolled + 3.

## Relaxed Tavern Rest
Completing a Long Rest in a comfortable tavern or a safe, warm shelter grants each character `1d8 Temporary Hit Points` lasting until the next sunset.
